An educational coloring and activity book that travels to 27 countries to show how families around the world celebrate autumn: harvest festivals, days of remembrance, kite and lantern festivals and thanksgiving traditions, from Día de los Muertos in Mexico to Obon in Japan. Each country has three pages: a country and tradition page with a flag and symbol to color, a symbols-and-words page with local words and a mini activity, and a scene page where children in regional dress join the celebration. A gentle, never-scary way to enjoy fall, printed on large 8.5 × 11 inch pages.
