A friendly little alien named Yupzi lands in Roswell, New Mexico, and sets off on a road trip through all 50 states. Each state gets two pages: a state map to color, with the capital, Yupzi's flight path and one fun fact, and a landmark scene to color with Yupzi in the picture. Printed single-sided on large 8.5 × 11 inch pages, with a travel map to check off and bonus pages to design your own alien.
